About us

Electronics you can buy with confidence.

We are a local electronics shop. This site is the system our team uses every day to keep track of what we have in stock and what we sell.

Who we are

Chidosky Electronics sells electronics to customers and businesses in our community. We want every customer to leave with the right product, a fair price and someone to call if something goes wrong.

Behind the counter, we used to count stock by hand and write sales in a book. Mistakes crept in, so we built this inventory system. Now every product, every stock movement and every sale is recorded in one place.

What we care about

A few simple rules we try to follow.

Honest products

We say what an item is and what it isn't, and we keep our records straight so the stock you see is the stock we have.

Fair prices

Prices are set once in the system, so the price on the shelf and the price at the till always match.

Help after the sale

Questions about an order or an account? Our support page and contact details are always one click away.
