Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

I created an account but I can't sign in.

New employee accounts have to be approved by the administrator before they work. Ask the administrator to enable your account on the Employees page, then try again.

I didn't get the confirmation email.

Check your spam or junk folder first. It can take a few minutes. If it isn't there, try to sign in: when your email isn't confirmed yet, the sign-in page offers to send the link again. Make sure you typed your email address correctly when you registered.

How do I change my name or password?

Open "My account" (the person icon at the top of your workspace, or the link at the bottom of the admin menu). You can change the name shown on your sales and choose a new password there.

How do I record a sale?

Sign in, pick the product from the list, set the quantity and press "Record sale". The stock goes down automatically and the sale appears in "My recent sales". Press "Print receipt" right afterwards if the customer wants one.

My sales are not showing in my list.

You only see the sales you recorded yourself, newest first. Sales made before your account was set up, or by someone else, won't be there. If a sale you just recorded is missing, refresh the page and tell the administrator so it can be checked.

A product shows "Out of stock". Can I still sell it?

No. The system won't let a sale go through if there isn't enough stock. Ask the administrator to add stock first.

Can I change the colours or switch to dark mode?

Yes. Use the moon or sun button at the top of any page. Your choice is remembered on that device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, every page is made to fit small screens. If something looks cut off, tell us which page and which phone you use.
